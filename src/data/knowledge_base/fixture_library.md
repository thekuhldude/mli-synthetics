# Fixture Library for Stage Lighting

This is a comprehensive reference of fixture types used in professional stage lighting design.

## 1. MOVING HEAD BEAM
**Description:** Tight, parallel light beam (4-8° angle). Razor-sharp.
**Capabilities:** Pan (540°), Tilt (270°), Dimmer (0-100%), Color Wheel (8-16 colors), Gobo Wheel (12+ gobos), Prism, Frost, Strobe
**Cannot:** Soft wash, audience blinding, large coverage
**Typical Position:** Truss (FOH front), Mid-stage truss, Floor positions facing up
**Best for:** Aerial effects, parallel beam looks, beam fans, audience scanning at high BPM electronic music
**Avoid for:** Acoustic music, ballads (too aggressive), low-energy moments
**Music Pairing:**
- High amounts (8-32 units): EDM, House, Techno, Drum & Bass, Metal
- Low amounts (2-4 units): Pop, Rock for accent moments
- Almost none: Acoustic, Singer-Songwriter, Classical
**Behavior Patterns:**
- Drops/Builds: Fast pan/tilt movements, color changes on beat
- Verse: Static beams, slow color shifts
- Chorus: Synchronized movement, wider position variations

## 2. MOVING HEAD SPOT
**Description:** Medium beam (10-25°), focused but versatile.
**Capabilities:** Pan/Tilt, Dimmer, Color Mixing (CMY/RGB), Gobos, Iris, Zoom (limited), Focus, Prism
**Cannot:** Wash entire stage, true RGBW mixing (older models)
**Typical Position:** Front truss, side trusses, sometimes backline
**Best for:** Key lighting on performers, gobo projections, mid-range coverage, theatrical work
**Avoid for:** Pure aerial beam work (too soft), full stage washes
**Music Pairing:**
- High amounts: Rock, Pop, Live Bands, Theater, Musicals
- Medium amounts: Hip-Hop, Jazz, Most Genres
- Low: Pure EDM (use Beams instead)
**Behavior Patterns:**
- Featured artist: Static spot on singer
- Solos: Follow the soloist
- Choruses: Synchronized gobo patterns

## 3. MOVING HEAD WASH (Wash Light)
**Description:** Wide beam (15-60° zoom), soft edges, used for color washes.
**Capabilities:** Pan/Tilt, Dimmer, RGBW Color Mixing, Zoom, sometimes Strobe
**Cannot:** Sharp gobos, beam effects, focused projection
**Typical Position:** Overhead truss, side positions, sometimes floor
**Best for:** Color washes, atmospheric lighting, blending, background color
**Avoid for:** Aerial effects, gobo work, precise positioning
**Music Pairing:**
- High amounts (12-40 units): Almost ALL genres, foundation of any rig
- Medium: Every show needs some washes
- Low: Rare to have none
**Behavior Patterns:**
- Verses: Slow color fades, gentle movement
- Drops: Fast color changes, bumps in intensity
- Breakdowns: Single color, very slow

## 4. LED PAR / STATIC WASH
**Description:** Fixed position RGB/RGBW LED wash. Cheap, common.
**Capabilities:** Dimmer, RGB(W) Color, sometimes Strobe, sometimes Zoom
**Cannot:** Movement, gobos, complex effects
**Typical Position:** Floor (stage edge), backline (uplight), side stage
**Best for:** Foundation color washes, uplighting performers, backline color
**Avoid for:** Anything requiring movement or sharp beams
**Music Pairing:**
- High amounts: Every show, foundation lighting
- Especially: Indie, Rock, Acoustic shows (static, atmospheric)
**Behavior Patterns:**
- Verses: Static colors
- Builds: Slow brightness increase
- Drops: Color change snap

## 5. BLINDER (Audience Blinder)
**Description:** Very bright warm white light pointed at audience. 2-lite, 4-lite, or 8-lite arrays.
**Capabilities:** Dimmer, sometimes Strobe (DWE-style)
**Cannot:** Color (mostly warm white only), movement
**Typical Position:** Truss facing audience, sometimes side stage
**Best for:** Drop moments, "swell" effects, build-ups, audience interaction
**Avoid for:** Mood lighting, color work, anything subtle
**Music Pairing:**
- High amounts (4-8 units): EDM Drops, Rock Choruses, Hip-Hop builds
- Low: Acoustic, Theater
**Behavior Patterns:**
- Build-up: Slow swell from 0 to 100%
- Drop: Snap on full, then strobe
- Verse: Off completely

## 6. STROBE LIGHT
**Description:** Ultra-fast flashing white (or RGB) light. Creates "frozen motion" effect.
**Capabilities:** Flash rate (1-30Hz), Intensity, sometimes Color (modern LED), sometimes Zones
**Cannot:** Sustained light, color subtlety
**Typical Position:** Truss, side stage, sometimes integrated in moving heads
**Best for:** EDM drops, Rock breakdowns, Metal breakdowns, accent moments
**Avoid for:** Anything sustained, low-energy music, anyone with photosensitive epilepsy (always warn!)
**Music Pairing:**
- High amounts: Hardstyle, Hardcore, Heavy Metal (during breakdowns), Drum & Bass
- Medium: EDM, Big Room House
- Low: Pop, Rock (accent only)
- None: Acoustic, Theater
**Behavior Patterns:**
- Drop hit: Single hard strobe burst (50-200ms)
- Sustained drop: Continuous strobing at song BPM/2
- Breakdown: No strobe

## 7. PIXEL BAR / LED BATTEN
**Description:** Linear LED strip with individually controllable pixels.
**Capabilities:** Per-pixel RGB(W), Chase patterns, Dimmer, sometimes Pan/Tilt (modern models)
**Cannot:** Sharp beams, gobos, large area coverage from single fixture
**Typical Position:** Truss edges, stage edge, between performers, vertical positions
**Best for:** Chase effects, pattern animations, futuristic looks, vertical accent
**Avoid for:** Soft wash lighting, classical theater
**Music Pairing:**
- High amounts: EDM (especially big room/festival), K-Pop, modern Pop
- Medium: Modern Rock, Hip-Hop
- Low: Acoustic, Classical
**Behavior Patterns:**
- Drop: Fast chase patterns synced to BPM
- Build: Symmetrical patterns building outward
- Breakdown: Single color, slow gradient

## 8. FOLLOWSPOT (manual or automated)
**Description:** High-intensity spotlight that follows a performer.
**Capabilities:** Manual operator control, sometimes color, iris, dimmer
**Cannot:** Automated patterns without operator, washes
**Typical Position:** FOH (front of house) high position, sometimes side
**Best for:** Featured performer key lighting, solos, ballads, theater
**Avoid for:** Group lighting, party music (no need)
**Music Pairing:**
- High importance: Theater, Musicals, Pop with featured singer
- Medium: Rock concerts, Acoustic
- Low: EDM, House (no central performer)
**Behavior Patterns:**
- Always on featured performer when present
- Slight dim during background/instrumental sections
- Off when performer leaves stage

## 9. SMOKE/HAZE MACHINE (not a light, but critical)
**Description:** Atmospheric effect. Hazer = thin, persistent. Smoke = thick, brief.
**Capabilities:** Output level, fan speed
**Cannot:** Light itself - only makes other lights visible as beams
**Typical Position:** Hidden on stage, sometimes audience
**Best for:** Making beam fixtures visible, atmospheric depth
**Critical Note:** WITHOUT HAZE, BEAM FIXTURES LOOK LIKE NOTHING. Always include haze if using Beams/Spots.
**Music Pairing:**
- Always on for: EDM, Rock, Metal, Hip-Hop
- Sometimes for: Theater (theatrical fog moments)
- Never for: Outdoor festivals (wind), some venues with smoke alarms

## 10. LASER (Class 3R/3B/4)
**Description:** Coherent light beams, very sharp and bright.
**Capabilities:** Beam patterns, scanning, color (modern RGB lasers)
**Cannot:** Wash lighting, audience contact (safety!)
**Typical Position:** Above audience (over heads), truss positions
**Best for:** EDM drops, atmospheric mid-air effects
**Avoid for:** Anything with risk of audience contact, smaller venues without proper safety
**Music Pairing:**
- High: Trance, Psytrance, Hard Dance, Big Room
- Medium: Most electronic music
- Low/None: Rock, Acoustic, Theater
**Behavior Patterns:**
- Build: Single beam slowly fanning
- Drop: Full pattern projection, multiple beams
- Verse: Off or single beam

## 11. CO2 JET / CRYO JET
**Description:** White CO2 plume shooting upward. Visual punctuation.
**Capabilities:** On/off only, sometimes duration control
**Cannot:** Sustained effect, color
**Typical Position:** Front of stage, audience-facing
**Best for:** Drop moments, big chorus hits, climactic moments
**Avoid for:** Sustained use, acoustic music
**Music Pairing:** Same as Blinders – drop moments only

## 12. PYRO (Pyrotechnics)
**Description:** Sparks, flames, gerbs. Real fire.
**Capabilities:** Single shot, sometimes timed sequences
**Cannot:** Anything other than predetermined effect
**Typical Position:** Stage front, sometimes side, requires safety distance
**Best for:** ONE moment per song max. Climactic peaks only.
**Safety Note:** Requires licensed operator, permits, fire department clearance. Don't simulate too often.
**Music Pairing:** Rock anthems, Metal anthems, Big Festival moments – ALWAYS sparse

## GENERAL RULES FOR FIXTURE DISTRIBUTION

### By Music Genre:

**EDM / Electronic (House, Techno, Trance, DnB):**
- 40% Moving Beams
- 25% Moving Washes
- 15% LED Bars/Pixel
- 10% Strobes
- 5% Blinders
- 5% Lasers + atmospherics

**Rock / Metal:**
- 30% Moving Spots
- 25% Moving Washes
- 20% LED Pars (uplight)
- 10% Blinders
- 10% Strobes (for breakdowns)
- 5% Special effects

**Pop / Hip-Hop:**
- 25% Moving Spots
- 25% Moving Washes
- 20% Moving Beams
- 15% LED Pars
- 10% Pixel Bars
- 5% Blinders

**Acoustic / Singer-Songwriter:**
- 40% LED Pars (static atmospheric)
- 30% Moving Spots (for performer)
- 20% Moving Washes (subtle color)
- 10% Followspot
- 0% Strobes, Beams, Lasers

**Theater / Musical:**
- 35% Moving Spots
- 25% LED Pars
- 20% Followspots
- 15% Moving Washes
- 5% Specials

### By Venue Size:

**Small Club (50-200 people):**
- 8-20 total fixtures
- No lasers, minimal beams
- Focus on washes and color

**Medium Club (200-1000 people):**
- 20-50 fixtures
- Add moving heads, some beams
- LED bars for accent

**Large Venue / Arena (1000-10000):**
- 50-150 fixtures
- Full rig with all categories
- Multiple positions

**Festival / Stadium (10000+):**
- 100-400 fixtures
- Massive beam counts
- Multiple lighting zones
- Heavy atmospheric

### Position Guidelines:

**Front Truss (FOH):**
- Moving Spots (for performers)
- Some Beams (audience effects)
- Followspot positions

**Mid Truss:**
- Moving Beams (most useful position)
- Moving Washes (color from above)

**Back Truss:**
- Moving Beams (backlight, beam fans)
- Strobes (counter-strobe to FOH)

**Stage Floor:**
- LED Pars (uplighting)
- Pixel Bars (vertical accent)
- Sometimes Moving Beams (looking up)

**Backline / Upstage:**
- LED Pars (backdrop color)
- Pixel Bars (vertical lines)

### Color Logic:

**Warm colors (red, orange, amber):**
- Energy, anger, passion
- Use in: Drops, choruses, energetic moments
- Pair with: Loud music, fast BPM, distortion

**Cool colors (blue, cyan, purple):**
- Calm, sad, mysterious
- Use in: Verses, breakdowns, ballads
- Pair with: Slow music, minor keys, atmospheric sections

**White:**
- Clarity, climax, neutrality
- Use in: Peak moments, key lighting
- Pair with: Big hits, dramatic reveals

**Green:**
- Tension, alien, unsettling
- Use sparingly: Builds, transitions, special moments
- Pair with: Dissonant music, build-ups

**Magenta/Pink:**
- Modern, pop, dreamy
- Use in: Pop music, dream sequences
- Pair with: Synth music, romantic ballads

## ANTI-PATTERNS (What NOT to do)

- Don't use strobes longer than 4-8 bars continuously (epilepsy risk + visual fatigue)
- Don't use lasers without haze (invisible)
- Don't change colors faster than 1/8th note (visual mush)
- Don't use blinders during verses (wastes the moment)
- Don't make every fixture move at once (chaos, not design)
- Don't ignore the music structure (lights should support, not fight)
- Don't use the same color for an entire song (boring)
- Don't sync everything to the kick drum only (one-dimensional)
